Shows selected airspace and only its touching or immediately adjacent neighbors (within 1.5 km). Frequencies are read from the bundled dataset; confirm current sector assignment, activation and procedures using the current AIP, NOTAM and responsible ATS unit.
Monitored Airspace
ENR operational model: published limits describe the available volume, not its current status. Confirm actual TRA/TSA activation with the responsible ATS unit; received real-time status is valid for 15 minutes. TRA GA ceilings must be entered from the coordinated approval. Open bundled ENR 1 ↗
Before marking a volume active: verify its identification, UTC start/end, approved vertical limits, QNH when applicable, and the responsible unit. Planned AUP/UUP use is not proof of real activation.

TRA: other traffic may transit only with ATC clearance. TSA: other traffic may not transit while active.
